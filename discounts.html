TouchBistro

Securing a Restaurant with 10+ Locations through Advanced Discounts

Restaurant POS Discounts

Role

Senior Product Designer

Timeline

Q2 – Q3 2023

Platform

iOS · Web

Overview

TouchBistro is a restaurant POS and management platform used by thousands of venues across North America. Multi-location restaurant groups needed a way to create, manage, and apply discounts consistently across all their locations — without requiring staff to manually configure each site.

I led design end-to-end on a new Advanced Discounts feature that allowed restaurant operators to define complex discount rules once and deploy them across 10+ locations simultaneously.

The Challenge

Restaurant groups with multiple locations struggled to maintain discount consistency. Managers had to log in to each POS individually to set up promotions, leading to errors, out-of-sync pricing, and lost revenue. The existing discount system wasn't designed for scale.

The core design problem: how do you make a powerful, rule-based discount engine feel simple enough for a busy restaurant manager to configure in under five minutes?

The Approach

I started with contextual interviews across five multi-location operators to understand their mental model around promotions. Key insight: managers think in terms of "event + condition + reward" — not the technical rule logic the backend required.

I designed a guided creation flow that mapped natural language discount types (Happy Hour, Staff Meal, Loyalty) to the underlying rule engine, hiding complexity behind familiar patterns. Location selection was reframed as a broadcast model — configure once, push everywhere.

Multiple rounds of usability testing with restaurant managers drove key simplifications: reducing a 12-step setup to 4 screens and introducing a live preview of how the discount would appear on a guest check.
