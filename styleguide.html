Comparison Cards

Two-column before/after or old/new comparison. Each card has a pill tag, subtitle, and a checklist of items.

Before

Users had to navigate three separate flows to complete a single task.

  • No clear entry point for new users
  • Duplicated steps across screens
After

A unified flow guides users from start to completion in one session.

  • Progressive disclosure reduces cognitive load
  • Inline validation prevents errors before submission
