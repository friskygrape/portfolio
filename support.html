TouchBistro

Reducing Customer Call Times by 33% by Improving the Information Architecture

Support Call Time Reduction IA

Role

Senior Product Designer

Timeline

Q4 2022 – Q1 2023

Platform

Web · iOS

Overview

TouchBistro's support team was handling a high volume of calls from restaurant operators who couldn't find answers within the product. Average call handle time had grown to over 8 minutes, straining the support organisation and delaying resolutions for customers.

I led an information architecture overhaul — restructuring the navigation, in-app help, and error messaging — that reduced average call times by 33% within two months of launch.

The Challenge

Support call transcripts revealed a pattern: most calls started with the phrase "I can't find where to…". The product had grown organically over several years and its navigation reflected that — features buried under inconsistent labels, settings scattered across unrelated sections.

Operators were calling support not because the features didn't exist, but because they couldn't locate them. The design problem was one of findability, not functionality.

The Approach

I ran a card sorting study with 24 operators across different restaurant types to uncover their mental model of the product. The results revealed four clear clusters that didn't match the existing nav structure at all.

Working with engineering and support leads, I restructured the navigation into five top-level areas based on operator jobs-to-be-done. I introduced contextual help tooltips tied to the top 20 support call topics, and redesigned error messages to include actionable next steps rather than generic codes.

A tree testing study validated the new structure before a single line of code was written, giving stakeholders the confidence to ship the full restructure in one release.
