TELUS

Growing Optik TV Daily Orders from 200 to 2,500+

TV Subscription Mobile Self-Service

Role

Senior Product Designer

Timeline

Q1 – Q3 2021

Platform

iOS · Android

Overview

TELUS Optik TV is a fibre-based television subscription service available to millions of Canadians. Despite strong brand awareness and an established customer base, the self-service ordering experience within the My TELUS app was underperforming — generating just 200 daily orders against a significantly higher ceiling.

I redesigned the end-to-end TV subscription flow, growing daily self-serve orders from 200 to over 2,500 — a 12× increase — within six months of launch.

The Challenge

The existing ordering flow required customers to navigate through five separate screens before reaching a package selection — and package options were presented as a wall of text with no visual hierarchy. Drop-off at each step compounded, resulting in an overall conversion rate below 4%.

Qualitative research revealed a second issue: customers didn't understand what they were buying. Descriptions used internal jargon ("Optik Starter", "Premium Tier 3") that meant nothing to someone who just wanted sports and movies.

The Approach

I mapped the full ordering funnel against session recordings and identified the three highest-impact drop-off points. From there, I ran a series of rapid prototyping sprints — two days of design, two days of unmoderated usability testing — iterating on each high-drop section independently.

The redesigned flow consolidated five screens into two. Package names were rewritten around customer interests ("Sports & Live TV", "Movies & Streaming") and paired with visual previews of included channels. A persistent order summary in the checkout rail eliminated the "what am I paying for?" uncertainty that caused late-funnel abandonment.

I partnered closely with the content design team to reframe every CTA — replacing "Proceed" with specific confirmations like "Add Sports & Live TV — $29/mo" — which A/B testing showed increased tap rate by 22%.

Daily self-serve orders climbed from 200 to 2,500+ within the first six months post-launch. The project became TELUS's internal benchmark for self-serve conversion, and the pattern system developed was adopted across three other product lines.
